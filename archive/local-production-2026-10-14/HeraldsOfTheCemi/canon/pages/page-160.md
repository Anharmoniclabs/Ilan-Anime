# Manuscript PDF page 160

                       HERALDS OF THE CEMÍ



  It was the first plan I’d had that wasn’t about me. I didn’t know yet
what I’d ask any of them for.
  I got off at 167th and walked the last block with my hands in my
pockets, and I didn’t look up at the window across the street. It was
dark now, and nobody was in it.
