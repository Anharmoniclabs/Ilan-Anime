                         FOUNDERS’ NIGHT



  Then it came back.
  I put my hand on La Madre’s case. The coin in my pocket was warm,
and it stayed warm, and I held on to that.
  At the lectern, Vale stepped up and put both hands on its edges. The
house lights began to fall.
  “Neighbors,” said Adrian Vale.
  He let the word sit.
  I started counting.
