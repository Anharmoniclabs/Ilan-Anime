   “Go,” said my mother. “Come back. Bring me the smell of her
house.” She pushed the can across the table. “And you call me from the
river.”



Manny called while they were boarding Group 6.
   “Sofía says you’re leaving the country the same week there’s a free
dinner with the creepy guy.”
   “Manny, don’t go to that.”
   “I’m going to that.”
   “It’s a trap.”
   “It’s tacos. I’m six-three, JC. I’m big and only a little bit stupid, so
they’re going to underestimate me, which is the whole plan.” I could
hear him chewing something. “I’m bringing Sofía. She’s going to wear
a wire made of spreadsheets.”
   “If you see Mikey—”
   “I’ll say hi. I’ll bring him a plate.”
   “Manny.”
   “I heard you,” he said, more quietly. “Go see your grandmother. I
got this block.”
   My phone buzzed against my ear. An email, from an address I knew.

   From: Adrian Vale
   Neighbor, I hear you may be away Thursday. Mikey will save you a seat
   regardless.

I had told three people I was leaving. I hadn’t told any of them when.
   The gate agent said my name twice before I heard it.
