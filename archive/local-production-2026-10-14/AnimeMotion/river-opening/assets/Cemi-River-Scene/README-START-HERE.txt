This ZIP contains all completed current assets, not a completed animation.

and final edit are NOT included. The original checkpoint predates the current
character revisions; use the character images in this ZIP as the current designs.
Character sheets are concept/model references, not layered rigs or vector assets.
Background plates and keyframes are flattened PNGs. No image pixels were altered
during packaging. Remaining art consistency and transparent-edge cleanup may be
needed before production; these assets are not certified animation-ready.
