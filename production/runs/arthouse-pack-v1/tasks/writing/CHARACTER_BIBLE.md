## Amaia model and puppet construction brief

Directly inspected `research/archive_imports/river_2026_10_11/Cemi-River-Scene/01-Characters/01-Amaia.png`: front, profile, back, wide reaching pose and three face expressions. Visible features are warm bronze pigment, long dark textured curls tied low with pale cloth, strong brows, ivory shoulder wrap, rust skirt with ragged layered hems and bare feet. These are observations of an exploratory sheet, not proof of Luis’s pencil likeness. The sheet’s athletic body and dramatic reaching action should be reconciled against the older bible’s gauntness and this scene’s exhausted restraint, not silently treated as final.

For the new sheet: align eye line, chin, shoulders, waist, knees and soles across front / three-quarter / profile / back views. Supply neutral, counting, alarm-contained, grief and protective-focus heads at the same skull scale. Preserve curl mass rather than drawing random different strands each pose. The sheet’s wrap coverage changes between views; choose a coherent seam/overlap map only as a marked proposal and route it for approval. Do not mirror an asymmetrical costume to obtain the opposite view.

For a digital cutout: body root at pelvis, torso, neck, fixed-volume head, separate eye/brow/mouth replacements, back-hair/front-hair, upper/lower arms, hands, wrap folds and skirt hem. Child support pose needs a dedicated shoulder/elbow/hand drawing and a child layer with contact anchors at Amaia’s ribs and forearm. Maintain overlap padding inside joints. Eye blink and mouth changes can use replacements; turning, bracing and reaching require new drawings. A layered puppet file is a setup deliverable, not evidence that those actions animate.

## Shared production sheet requirements

Every sheet carries character ID, variant, take, source references and exploratory status in editorial margins; scene art has no generated labels. A pose library stores full-canvas PNGs with transparent background, common origin, integer exposure proposals and registration landmarks. Keep skin swatches under neutral viewing light separate from scene lighting; proposed ivory/rust garments are not a mandated universal Taíno costume. Stage characters as specific individuals rather than interchangeable “tribal” costumes or stock anime faces.

Hero close-ups need face/hand contact review at final output scale. Background banks need readable variation and correct ages. Full episode cast coverage in this bible does not imply finished model sheets or puppets for every role.
